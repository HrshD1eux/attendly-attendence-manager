Designed for Students

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built purely with native Android components — Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, DataStore, and Jetpack Glance widgets.

Today's Schedule Timeline

Visual chronological timeline connecting your classes for the day. Single-tap to mark Present, Absent, or Cancelled with instant undo support.

Safe Absences & Recovery

No guesswork. Computes the exact number of classes you can miss while staying above your target, and consecutive attendances required if you fall behind.

AI Timetable Text Importer

Turn messy PDFs or photo timetables into structured schedules. Includes ready-to-copy prompts for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude and an offline parser.

Interactive Home Widgets

Jetpack Glance widgets for your launcher. View your overall percentage at a glance and mark current class attendance with tactile P and A buttons.

Monthly Calendar History

Need to correct last Thursday's attendance? Easily jump to any date on the calendar, view historical sessions, and update status anytime.

SAF Backup & CSV Export

Export your full database to JSON backups or spreadsheet-ready CSV using Android's Storage Access Framework. 100% offline and user-controlled.

No Accounts. No Tracking. Pure Privacy.

Attendly does not ask for your email, phone number, or internet connection. Everything runs locally on your device with high-performance Room SQLite storage.
